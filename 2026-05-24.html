Anthropic

Claude Blog

Anthropic shipped three major upgrades to Claude Managed Agents: Dreaming, Outcomes, and multiagent orchestration. Dreaming lets agents review their own session history to extract patterns and curate memories between runs—essentially scheduled self-reflection. Outcomes adds a rubric-based grader that evaluates agent output in a separate context window, improving task success by up to 10 points on hard problems and boosting docx/pptx generation quality by 8–10%. Multiagent orchestration allows a lead agent to delegate parallel subtasks to specialists with their own models and tools, complete with persistent events and full traceability in the Claude Console. Early adopters include Harvey (6x completion rate improvement), Netflix (parallel log analysis), and Spiral by Every (Haiku lead + Opus writers with outcomes-based quality gates).

Today we're launching dreaming in Claude Managed Agents as a research preview. Dreaming extends memory by reviewing past sessions to find patterns and help agents self-improve.

The Takeaway: Google believes world models are the next layer after language, but the 'GPT moment' for pure visual understanding—extracting physics from video without text—hasn't arrived yet.
