Aaron Levie

CEO @ Box

Box CEO Aaron Levie articulated the thesis that model routing is an inevitable outcome as token consumption grows into a meaningful operating expense. He argues that enterprises won't be able to solve model routing at scale themselves — the products that intelligently dispatch workflows to the right model tier (frontier vs. cost-efficient) will aggregate the most demand. For now, most use cases still need frontier performance, but over time, as quality on smaller models catches up, companies will peel off individual use-cases to cheaper tiers. This is one of the clearest articulations yet of why the 'AI router' layer will be a critical piece of enterprise infrastructure.

As token budgets take on a larger part of operating expenses over time, model routing is the inevitable conclusion.
