Alfred Wahlforss

Founder & CEO of Listen Labs

The Takeaway: AI-first customer research is about to become the most important function in any product organization—because when building becomes easy, knowing what to build becomes the only thing that matters.

Alfred Wahlforss, founder and CEO of Listen Labs, is building an AI-native customer research platform that runs thousands of voice and video interviews simultaneously. Listen's AI agent conducts Zoom-like conversations with real humans—detecting emotion through facial expressions, voice tone, and body language—then aggregates insights across thousands of interviews. Wahlforss's insight is that surveys have a fundamental reliability problem: people are radically inconsistent when asked the same multiple-choice question twice. But when they have to reason through their answer in a conversation with an AI, consistency improves dramatically.

The platform already serves 20% of the Fortune 500, including Microsoft, Anthropic, Sweetgreen, and NBC. A Chubbies customer story illustrates the power: they discovered that chest hair interacted poorly with one of their shirt materials, changed the fabric, and radically improved comfort. Wahlforss also revealed a fascinating asymmetry: people are more honest with AI than with human interviewers, finding it a 'therapeutic experience' because the AI is a non-judgmental entity genuinely interested in them. This makes the platform particularly useful for sensitive research—interviewing children about products, for example.

Listen Labs' most ambitious feature is launching soon: simulation. After conducting tens of thousands of interviews, Listen aims to predict how customers will answer questions in the future—essentially a customer behavior simulator built from empirical interview data. The company's audience pool of 30 million participants, combined with 80% of engineering resources focused on audience stratification, means Listen can find exactly the right expert for any product question, from sneaker enthusiasts to oncologists.

As we get closer to AGI, it will be easier to build things, but the hard part will be knowing what to build. And that's what we're building at Listen.

Alfred Wahlforss

Founder & CEO of Listen Labs

核心要点：AI 驱动的客户研究即将成为任何产品组织中最重要的功能——因为当构建变得容易时，知道该构建什么就成了唯一重要的事情。

Listen Labs 创始人兼 CEO Alfred Wahlforss 正在构建一个 AI 原生的客户研究平台，能够同时运行数千个语音和视频访谈。Listen 的 AI Agent 与真实人类进行类似 Zoom 的对话——通过面部表情、语音语调 and 肢体语言检测情绪——然后汇总来自数千次访谈的洞察。Wahlforss 的洞见是，调查存在一个根本性的可靠性问题：当被问及同一个选择题两次时，人们的回答可能截然不同。但当他们需要在与 AI 的对话中推理自己的答案时，一致性会显著提高。

该平台已服务 20% 的财富 500 强企业，包括 Microsoft、Anthropic、Sweetgreen 和 NBC。Chubbies 的客户故事说明了其威力：他们发现胸毛与某种衬衫面料产生不良互动，于是更换了面料，大幅提升了舒适度。Wahlforss 还揭示了一个有趣的非对称性：人们在与 AI 交谈时比与人类访谈员更诚实，认为这是一种 '治疗性体验'，因为 AI 是一个真正对受访者感兴趣的非评判性实体。这使得该平台对敏感研究尤其有用——例如针对儿童的产品访谈。

Listen Labs 即将推出最具雄心的功能：模拟。在进行数万次访谈后，Listen 旨在预测客户未来将如何回答问题——本质上是一个基于经验访谈数据构建的客户行为模拟器。该公司拥有 3000 万参与者的受众池，并将 80% 的工程资源投入到受众分层上，这意味着 Listen 可以为任何产品问题找到完全合适的专家——从运动鞋爱好者到肿瘤学家。

As we get closer to AGI, it will be easier to build things, but the hard part will be knowing what to build. And that's what we're building at Listen.
